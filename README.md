## Our Ecosystem 🌐

Our strength lies in the interconnection of our B2B and impact solutions.

- **Horizon Cosmos:** Intelligent CRM with AI for sales optimization and relationship management.
- **Horizon Wave:** Fintech Payment Gateway and SDK for secure and easy integration.
- **Horizon Atlaas:** HR Tech suite that unifies the employee lifecycle (Onboarding, Performance, Culture).

Our startups focused on social impact and improving quality of life:
- **Senses:** Platform for alternative communication and development support for autistic children.

## Our Flagship Projects ✨

We highlight our key projects that illustrate our expertise across social impact, health, and enterprise SaaS:

### Project 1: [Senses]
- **Focus:** Inclusion and Child Development.
- **Description:** Mobile solution for Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) and routine management support for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

### Project 2: [ConectaVital]
- **Focus:** Digital Health and Predictive Monitoring.
- **Description:** Platform for 24/7 monitoring of chronic patients, integrating wearables and generating risk alerts for assisted intervention.

### Project 3: [PetGo]
- **Focus:** Specialized Pet Services and Well-being.
- **Description:** Platform connecting pet owners to specialized, safe, and sustainable transportation and pet-friendly services.

### Project 4: [Cosmos]
- **Focus:** Intelligent CRM and Sales Optimization.
- **Description:** Our core SaaS platform utilizing AI for smart lead scoring, automation, and holistic customer relationship management.

### Project 5: [Sonda]
- **Focus:** Environmental Security and Early Warning Systems.
- **Description:** System based on multimodal data and Machine Learning for the predictive alerting of natural disasters (e.g., floods, landslides).
